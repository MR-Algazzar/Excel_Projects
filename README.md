## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 002_Sales_Dashboard_2026
* **Overview:** Transforms raw transactional sales data into a clean, dynamic, executive-ready dashboard designed to give stakeholders real-time insights into revenue, profit, product performance, and regional growth.
* **Tools & Features:** Microsoft Excel, XLOOKUP, SUMIFS, Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, Connected Interactive Slicers, and Dynamic KPI Summary Cards.
* **Key Metrics & Insights:**
  * **KPI Summary Cards:** Tracks Total Sales , Gross Profit , Average Price , and Total Units Sold .
  * **Regional & Category Breakdown:** Evaluates geographical revenue distribution across Europe and identifies top-performing product categories.
  * **Time Trends & Bestsellers:** Analyzes monthly revenue vs. gross profit trends alongside the Top 5 Bestselling products.
